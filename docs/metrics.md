# Metrics & KPI Framework

## North Star

**Verified Decision Value (VDV)**

A composite measure of how much the platform improves the speed and quality of customer decisions.

## Core product metrics

- time from event ingestion to analyst visibility;
- time from alert to decision;
- percentage of alerts with complete provenance;
- false-positive rate;
- analyst acknowledgement rate;
- analyst override rate;
- investigation completion rate.

## AI metrics

- answer latency;
- cost per investigation;
- evidence coverage;
- hallucination/error rate;
- cross-agent disagreement;
- human acceptance rate.

## Commercial metrics

- qualified pilots;
- pilot-to-paid conversion;
- MRR / ARR;
- expansion revenue;
- churn;
- net revenue retention;
- CAC payback;
- gross margin.

## Enterprise metrics

- active organizations;
- active analysts;
- API calls;
- source connectors;
- SLA compliance;
- uptime;
- incident count.

## 90-day target example

| KPI | Target |
|---|---:|
| Design partners | 2–3 |

| Weekly active analysts | 10+ |
| Provenance coverage | >95% |
| Alert acknowledgement | >60% |
