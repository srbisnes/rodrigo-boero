# SWARM INTEL — Investor / Enterprise Pitch Deck

## Slide 1 — The thesis
**From fragmented global signals to decision-ready intelligence.**

AI-assisted decision intelligence for organizations exposed to geopolitical, infrastructure, supply-chain and macroeconomic risk.

## Slide 2 — The problem
Critical decisions are slowed by fragmented sources, alert overload and lack of context.

Teams need to know:
- what changed;
- why it matters;
- what is connected;
- what deserves attention now.

## Slide 3 — The product
**Detect → Reason → Correlate → Decide**

One operating picture combining data, specialist agents, evidence and workflows.

## Slide 4 — The demo
- global risk map;
- infrastructure view;
- economic indicators;
- six-agent swarm;
- evidence viewer;
- operational log.

## Slide 5 — Why agents
Different risk domains require different reasoning.

Six specialist agents analyze the same situation and a synthesis layer exposes agreement, conflict and priority.

## Slide 6 — Trust architecture
Every production observation carries source, timestamp, freshness, confidence and provenance.

AI assists. Humans remain accountable for high-impact actions.

## Slide 7 — Initial customers
- logistics and maritime;
- critical infrastructure;
- insurers;
- financial institutions;
- commodities;
- multinational operations.

## Slide 8 — Business model
- analyst/workspace SaaS;
- enterprise contracts;
- API usage;
- premium data connectors;
- managed intelligence workflows.

## Slide 9 — Roadmap
0–3: production foundation.

3–6: paid pilots.

6–12: enterprise SaaS.

12–18: proprietary risk models + ecosystem.

## Slide 10 — Moat
The moat is not the map.

It is the event schema, dependency graph, evaluation data, source reliability, customer workflows and historical decision outcomes.

## Slide 11 — Funding use
Prioritize engineering, source access, security, pilots, legal/compliance and customer acquisition.

Release capital against measurable milestones.

## Slide 12 — The ask
Build the infrastructure that turns global information into operational advantage.

**Next step:** design-partner pilot with measurable decision-time and alert-quality outcomes.
